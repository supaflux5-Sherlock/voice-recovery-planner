If you're in crisis right now

  • Call or text 988 — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, free and available 24/7. Texting works when speaking is hard.
  • Text HOME to 741741 — Crisis Text Line, 24/7, text-only.
  • Call 911 or go to your nearest ER for a medical emergency.

More support

  • American Cancer Society 24/7 Helpline — 1-800-227-2345
  • CancerCare (free counseling & support groups) — 1-800-813-4673
  • NAMI HelpLine (mental health, any cause) — 1-800-950-6264
  • WebWhispers — free online community for laryngectomees (webwhispers.org)
  • SPOHNC — Support for People with Oral and Head and Neck Cancer (spohnc.org)

This app doesn't replace professional care. If you're worried, reach out to your care team or one of the resources above.
